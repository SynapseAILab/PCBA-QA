# PCBA-QA



## Project Motivation and Objectives

The Necessity of Introducing AI Teaching: Constructing a 24/7 Intelligent Mentor System.

In hands-on lab environments, due to the precision of production line equipment and complex operation steps, instructors often struggle to simultaneously attend to every student's operational details. To address this teaching pain point, this project plans to introduce an AI voice-guided robot to construct an optimized "student-to-teacher ratio" teaching environment:

- **Real-time operation guidance:** Students can obtain standardized operating guidelines for various precision equipment (such as SMT placement machines and reflow ovens) through the AI robot, thereby alleviating instructors' guidance pressure.
- **On-demand Q&A feedback:** The AI system can instantly provide precise answers to operational questions, ensuring that students can troubleshoot obstacles at any time when operating the production lines of the "PCBA Smart Manufacturing Talent Training Base".
